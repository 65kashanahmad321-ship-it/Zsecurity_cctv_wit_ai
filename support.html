Common Inquiries

How do I access my cloud recordings? expand_more
You can access up to 30 days of encrypted footage via the ZSecurity Mobile App or the Web Portal using your authorized Master Credentials. If you are locked out, contact the emergency hotline.
What happens during a power outage? expand_more
Our enterprise nodes are equipped with UPS backup systems that maintain recording and AI functionality for up to 12 hours. The system will alert you via SMS the moment main power drops.
Can I upgrade my AI detection modules? expand_more
Yes, facial recognition, license plate reading, and thermal threat detection can be added to compatible V4 Ghost Eye cameras via a remote firmware deployment. Contact the sales desk to provision the upgrade.
